**Tradebot**: memecoin trading bot for your FOMO wallet and Liquid, with a dashboard. It starts in paper mode (practice money, live prices).

- **Windows:** download `Tradebot-Windows.exe` and double-click it. Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app is not signed: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Keep the black window open while the bot runs.
- **Mac:** download `Tradebot-macOS`, then in Terminal run `chmod +x ~/Downloads/Tradebot-macOS && ~/Downloads/Tradebot-macOS`. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose **Open Anyway**.

The dashboard opens at http://localhost:8787. Your settings, keys and trade journal are kept in the `Tradebot` folder in your home folder. To connect FOMO, open **Connections** in the dashboard and follow the steps there. Never paste your key anywhere else.
